# Professional Portfolio Programs

This ledger separates **published studies** from **reviewed and deferred
programs**. A project is not promoted to a public case study merely because a
working folder, draft package, or attractive concept exists. Public material
requires evidence that supports the stated lifecycle, a clear sanitization
boundary, and a reviewable explanation that does not expose private operations.

## Published studies

### Data Contract Monitor

The published study explains fail-closed contract drift governance: stable
contract identity, independent rule evaluation, evidence quality, impact
explanation, review state, and versioned change control. It is informed by an
accepted working/save-state baseline while the private package and data remain
owner-only.

### Data Governance & Lineage Portal

The published study explains stable asset identity, provenance, lineage,
stewardship, conflict visibility, publication state, and rollback. It is
informed by a verified maintenance known-good baseline with a distinct
field-confirmed rollback while private catalog and deployment evidence remain
owner-only.

## Reviewed and deferred programs

### Workflow and Case Management Platform

Deferred until the exact candidate package, Recovery Doctor behavior, native
Windows acceptance, sanitization review, and fresh support evidence justify a
truthful public study.

### Policy and Procedure Navigator

Deferred until the accepted package and evaluation evidence demonstrate
evidence-grounded answers, citation behavior, permission boundaries, controlled
workflow handling, and a public-safe corpus.

### PC Reliability & Incident Intelligence Suite

Deferred until collectors, incident correlation, redaction, performance,
Windows acceptance, and bounded diagnostic evidence are verified without
publishing machine-specific or security-sensitive details.

### Operations Intelligence & Automation Platform

Deferred until its dataset contract, metrics, dashboard logic, anomaly and
root-cause evidence, workflow recommendations, and downloadable reports have an
accepted package and public-safe demonstration.

## Publication gate

A deferred program can move to published only when the available evidence
supports all of the following:

1. A stable project identity and professional public name.
2. A clearly classified current, candidate, rollback, or source-only state.
3. Accepted or explicitly bounded verification evidence.
4. A public-safe synthetic scenario with no private data or operational access.
5. A source and rights review that preserves third-party terms.
6. Automated checks for broken links and sensitive or operational residue.
7. A truthful limitations section that does not claim deployment, production
   safety, certification, profitability, or platform endorsement.

## Public boundary

This repository publishes documentation and synthetic reasoning only. It does
not contain operational packages, executables, credentials, private
configuration, production datasets, customer or employee records, private
catalogs, proprietary strategy, machine details, support exports, package
digests, Drive identifiers, or deployment commands.

The ledger cannot promote a private package, connect to a production system,
change metadata, enforce a data contract, answer from a private policy corpus,
repair a computer, or assign a real case. Project-specific working, candidate,
rollback, and acceptance evidence remains under its separate authority.
